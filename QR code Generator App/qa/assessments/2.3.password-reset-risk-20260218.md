# Risk Assessment: 2.3.password-reset.md

## Overview
This document identifies and evaluates risks associated with the Password Reset feature as described in story 2.3.password-reset.md.

## Risk Matrix
| Risk ID | Description | Probability | Impact | Risk Level | Mitigation |
|---------|-------------|-------------|--------|------------|------------|
| R1 | Email with reset link intercepted or sent to wrong user | Medium | High | High | Use secure token, expire tokens quickly, verify email ownership |
| R2 | Weak password policy allows easy-to-guess passwords | Low | High | Medium | Enforce strong password policy, validate on reset |
| R3 | Token reuse or replay attack | Low | High | Medium | Invalidate token after use, single-use tokens |
| R4 | Expired or invalid token not handled gracefully | Medium | Medium | Medium | Show clear error, allow re-request |
| R5 | Email service failure prevents reset | Medium | High | High | Monitor email service, provide fallback or support |
| R6 | Brute force attempts on reset endpoint | Low | High | Medium | Rate limit reset requests, CAPTCHA |
| R7 | Logging sensitive information (e.g., tokens, emails) | Low | High | Medium | Mask sensitive data in logs |
| R8 | UI/UX confusion leads to user lockout | Medium | Medium | Medium | Clear instructions, error messages, support contact |

## Summary
The password reset flow is a high-value target for attackers. Key mitigations include secure token handling, strong password enforcement, robust error handling, and monitoring of email delivery. Regular review and testing of the reset process are recommended.
