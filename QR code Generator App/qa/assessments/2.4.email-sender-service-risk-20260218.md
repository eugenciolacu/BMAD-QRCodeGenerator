## Critical Risks Requiring Immediate Attention

### 1. SEC-001: Exposure of Email Credentials

**Score: 9 (Critical)**
**Probability**: High - Developers may accidentally commit credentials or misconfigure secrets management.
**Impact**: High - Compromised Gmail account, data breach, or service disruption.
**Mitigation**:
- Store credentials in secure secrets store (not in source control)
- Enforce code review for credential handling
- Add automated checks for secrets in repo
  **Testing Focus**: Attempt to send emails with invalid/missing credentials, check for secrets in repo
